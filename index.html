Multimodal Knowledge Distiller

2. Knowledge Distillation Loss: Uses a weighted combination of KL-Divergence soft-target loss and hard-label cross-entropy loss to guide student model learning[cite: 5].

3. Parameter Optimization: Significantly compresses model parameters to enable faster inference and lower resource utilization on edge or constrained deployments[cite: 5].
